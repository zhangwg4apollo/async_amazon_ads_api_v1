# Search (Impression share)

The Search metric family contains 3 fields, listed below:

- **Program eligibility**: [Sponsored Products](guides/sponsored-products/overview), [Sponsored Brands](guides/sponsored-brands/overview)
- **Description**: The numeric rank of your account-wide impression share compared to all other advertisers.
