# Impressions (Delivery)

The Impressions metric family contains 6 fields, listed below:

## Impressions

- **Reporting Field ID**: `metric.impressions`
- **Data Type**: `LONG`
- **Program eligibility**: [Sponsored Products](guides/sponsored-products/overview), [Sponsored Brands](guides/sponsored-brands/overview), [Sponsored Display](guides/sponsored-display/overview), [Sponsored TV](guides/sponsored-tv/overview), [Amazon DSP](guides/dsp/developer-guide)
