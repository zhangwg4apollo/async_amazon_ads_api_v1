# Incremental reach (linear TV) (Reach)

The Incremental reach (linear TV) metric family contains 2 fields, listed below:

## Exclusive reach rate (linear TV)

- **Reporting Field ID**: `metric.exclusiveReachRateLinearTV`
- **Data Type**: `PERCENTAGE`
- **Program eligibility**: [Sponsored Display](guides/sponsored-display/overview), [Sponsored TV](guides/sponsored-tv/overview), [Amazon DSP](guides/dsp/developer-guide)
- **Description**: The percentage of streaming TV reach that wasn't exposed to a linear TV campaign. Exclusive reach rate (linear TV) = 100 x (streaming TV reach - linear TV duplicates) / streaming TV reach.
- **Required fields**: `campaign.id` [📎](guides/reporting/ads-v1/dimensions/level-of-detail/campaign#campaign-id-primary-key)
- **Complementary fields**: N/A

## Incremental reach rate (linear TV)

- **Reporting Field ID**: `metric.incrementalReachRateLinearTV`
- **Data Type**: `PERCENTAGE`
- **Program eligibility**: [Sponsored Display](guides/sponsored-display/overview), [Sponsored TV](guides/sponsored-tv/overview), [Amazon DSP](guides/dsp/developer-guide)
- **Description**: The percentage of unique households reached on streaming TV compared to linear TV. Households included in linear TV reach aren't counted in streaming TV reach. Incremental reach rate (linear TV) = 100 x (streaming TV reach - linear TV duplicates) / linear TV reach
