# Content title (Supply)

The Content title dimension contains 1 field, listed below:

## Content title (Primary Key)

- **Reporting Field ID**: `contentTitle.value`
- **Data Type**: `STRING`
- **Description**: The name of the video or audio.
- **Required fields**: N/A
- **Complementary fields**: N/A
- **Version 3 Reporting Name (DSP)**: `contentTitle`
- **Version 3 Reporting Name (Sponsored Ads)**: N/A
