# Converted product marketplace (Product)

The Converted product marketplace dimension contains 1 field, listed below:

## Converted product marketplace (Primary Key)

- **Reporting Field ID**: `convertedProductMarketplace.value`
- **Data Type**: `STRING`
- **Description**: The marketplace where the conversion event for the attributed product occurred.
- **Required fields**: N/A
- **Complementary fields**: N/A
- **Version 3 Reporting Name (DSP)**: `marketplace`
- **Version 3 Reporting Name (Sponsored Ads)**: `marketplace`
