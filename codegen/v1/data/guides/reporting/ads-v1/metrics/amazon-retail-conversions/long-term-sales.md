# Long-term sales (Amazon retail conversions)

The Long-term sales metric family contains 3 fields, listed below:

## Long-term sales

- **Reporting Field ID**: `metric.longTermSales`
- **Data Type**: `CURRENCY`
- **Program eligibility**: [Sponsored Brands](guides/sponsored-brands/overview), [Sponsored Display](guides/sponsored-display/overview), [Sponsored TV](guides/sponsored-tv/overview), [Amazon DSP](guides/dsp/developer-guide), Full-Funnel
